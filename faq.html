Accounts and getting started

Is KonfluenX free?

Yes. Accounts, signals, community, leaderboards and courses cost nothing. We earn through Deriv's official partner programme rather than charging traders.

Do I need a Deriv account?

To trade, yes. To browse markets, read signals and take part in the community, no. You can connect Deriv whenever you are ready, from the dashboard or from Settings.

Can I use KonfluenX without trading at all?

Absolutely. Plenty of people use it for the market rooms, the analysis and the courses.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account Settings → Delete account. Your profile, posts and comments are removed. Signals you published are kept in anonymised aggregate form so that other users' historical statistics stay accurate. This does not close your Deriv account.

Connecting Deriv

Is it safe? Do you see my password?

No, never. Connection uses Deriv's own OAuth flow: you sign in on Deriv's site and grant specific permissions. We receive an encrypted access token, never your password. You can revoke it from Settings or from Deriv at any time.

What permissions do you ask for?

Read access to your account details and balance, and trading permission so we can place the orders you approve. We ask only for what the features you use require, and a permission you decline disables its feature rather than breaking the app.

Can I connect more than one Deriv account?

Yes. Real and demo accounts under your Deriv login appear in the account switcher on every trading screen. Check which one is selected before you trade.

My balance looks wrong

The balance is a cached read with the fetch time shown underneath. Refresh the dashboard, or reconnect from Settings. Where KonfluenX and Deriv disagree, Deriv is correct.

Trading

What can I trade here?

Accumulators, Rise/Fall, Digits and Multipliers on supported synthetic indices, with more as Deriv's API allows. The platform checks what is genuinely executable per market before showing you a trade button.

Why can I not trade a particular signal?

It is an analysis signal — a trade idea with entry, stop loss and targets that does not map to an executable Deriv contract. It shows View Market instead. This is deliberate, not a bug.

The price changed between the quote and my purchase

Prices move continuously. The proposal you see is a quote at a moment in time; the contract opens at the price available when the order reaches Deriv. Deriv's record is authoritative.

Where do I see my open positions?

Trade → Open Contracts, or the Portfolio tab. Closed positions are in Trade History with the full result.

Can I close a position early?

Where Deriv supports it for that contract type — Multipliers and Accumulators generally do; fixed-duration contracts such as Rise/Fall generally do not. The Sell button appears only when it will actually work.

Signals and results

Who writes the signals?

Other users. KonfluenX does not author trading calls and does not guarantee anyone else's. See the Signal Disclaimer.

Can someone delete their losing signals?

No. Signals lock when published. Retracting one removes it from feeds but keeps it in the author's history and in the denominator of their win rate.

What does "Boosted" mean?

KonfluenX gave that signal more visibility for a limited period. It is not an endorsement and carries no guarantee about the outcome.

How do I become a signal provider?

Post signals. Any eligible account can, subject to a short probation period for new accounts and rate limits per market. Consistency and sample size are what build a profile worth following.

Why is a trader with 100% win rate not on the leaderboard?

Rankings require a minimum number of published signals. One winning signal is 100% and means nothing, so it does not qualify.

Deposits and withdrawals

Does KonfluenX hold my money?

No. There is no KonfluenX wallet and no KonfluenX balance. Funds live in your Deriv account.

What are the two deposit options?

Deriv takes you to Deriv's own cashier. Kaastro is an independent approved payment service — choosing it means leaving KonfluenX, and we show a full-screen notice before you do. Kaastro has its own verification, fees and processing times.

My deposit has not arrived

Deposits are processed by Deriv or Kaastro, not by us, so we cannot see or speed up a payment. Contact whichever you used. If a Deriv deposit has cleared but your KonfluenX balance is stale, refresh the dashboard.

Can I move money between my Deriv accounts?

Yes, from the Transfer screen. The transfer is executed by Deriv between accounts you own.

Installing the app

Is there an Android or iOS app?

KonfluenX is a web app you install rather than download from a store. Once installed it opens full screen from your home screen, works offline for cached content, and updates itself with no store review queue.

How do I install it?

Use the Install button on the homepage. Your browser handles the rest — KonfluenX then opens full screen from your home screen or desktop, like any other app.

Does it work offline?

Partly. The app shell and recently viewed content stay available offline so it opens instantly. Live prices, placing trades and posting all require a connection — for obvious reasons, we never serve you a cached price as if it were live.

How do I update it?

It updates itself. When a new version is ready you will see a prompt to reopen the app.

Safety and support

Someone is asking me to send them money

Report them immediately and do not send anything. Soliciting funds from other users is banned. KonfluenX staff will never ask you for money, your Deriv password or an API token.

How do I report a post, signal or user?

Use the Report option on the item, or email moderation@vybe.app. Reports are anonymous to the person reported.

I think I found a security bug

Please report it privately to security@vybe.app before disclosing publicly. We respond within two working days.

I need help I cannot find here

The contact page reaches a person, seven days a week.
