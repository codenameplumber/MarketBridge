Referral Revenue vs Referral Payouts

Top Referrers

Revenue is the platform fee KonfluenX collected from the referrer's users. Ties break on revenue, so sign-ups alone never top the board. Conversion needs at least 3 referrals.

Fraud & Controls

Programme Rules

Admins view, investigate, verify statuses and export. Rules, settlement and money adjustments are Super Admin only, and every change needs a reason.

Programme audit trail

Deriv pays affiliate commissions directly to the affiliate. That money belongs to the affiliate — it is not KonfluenX revenue, so no commission estimate appears here. KonfluenX tracks the onboarding pipeline and whatever performance data the affiliate chooses to share.

Affiliate pipeline

Users ask Deriv to link their account to the KonfluenX IB. Once the link shows in the Deriv partner dashboard, an admin verifies it here: the user gets a Partner Badge and earns KX Points on qualifying trading activity. KX Points are rewards, not cash.
