Why we built it

Synthetic indices attract a particular kind of trader — and the tools they are given treat every market as interchangeable.

These markets run 24/7, they behave in ways that reward pattern recognition, and the people who trade them specialise hard. A V75 trader is not a Boom 1000 trader, and neither of them trades Step Index the same way.

But the software does not know that. You get a chart, a stake box and a buy button. Want to know what other V75 traders are seeing? Leave, and go to a Telegram group. Want to learn Smart Money Concepts? Leave, and go to YouTube. Want to know whether the person selling signals is any good? Take their screenshot on faith.

KonfluenX closes that loop. Every market has a chart, a trading desk, its own signals, its own analysis, its own traders and its own conversation — and each one leads to the next.

What we are, precisely

We are an interface and a community layer on top of Deriv. That distinction decides almost everything else, so here it is without hedging.

We are not a broker
We do not accept deposits, hold client funds, or execute trades on our own book. Deriv does all three.
We are not a financial adviser
Nothing on the platform is a personal recommendation or investment advice.
We do not author trading signals
Signals and analysis come from users. We do not write trading calls or guarantee anyone else's.
We provide the experience
The trading interface, the market pages, the signals, the community, the discovery and the education.
We make the record trustworthy
Signals lock on publish and results resolve from evidence, so a win rate here means something.

What we will not do

A trading platform's character shows in what it refuses to build. Ours refuses four things, and each refusal cost us a feature somebody asked for.

Refusal 01

We will not hold your funds

There is no wallet in KonfluenX and no balance in our database. The figure on your dashboard is a timestamped read of your Deriv account, and when it goes stale we tell you rather than showing you a comfortable number. Deposits and withdrawals hand you to Deriv or to Kaastro, with a full-screen notice when you are leaving.

Refusal 02

We will not show a trade button that does not work

An idea like V75 buy, entry 542,800, stop 541,900, target 545,500 is a perfectly good trade idea. It is also not a Deriv contract, and no amount of interface design makes it one. So it appears as analysis, with a View Market button and the levels laid out honestly. Only signals that map to a contract we can genuinely configure and buy get Trade This Signal.

Refusal 03

We will not let anyone launder a track record

The obvious way to fake a 90% win rate is to post a hundred signals and delete the losers. Here a signal locks the moment it publishes. Deleting it retracts it from the feed but keeps it in the denominator, and it stays visible on the author's profile. Results come from the actual contract outcome or the price feed — never from the author saying so. And nobody enters a leaderboard until they have posted enough signals for the number to mean anything.

Refusal 04

We will not pretend a boost is an endorsement

We do promote selected signals to more people. That is a visibility decision, it is time-boxed, and it is labelled as one. It has never meant we think the trade will win, and the badge says so.

How we make money

Through Deriv's official partner programme, not by charging you. When you create or connect a Deriv account through KonfluenX and trade, Deriv pays us a partner commission under their own rules and their own attribution.

We prefer being clear about this. It means our incentive is to get the platform in front of more traders and keep them here — not to sell you a subscription, gate the community, or charge for signals.

It also means we are not neutral about you having a Deriv account. You should know that when you read anything we write about Deriv.

Our conflict of interest, stated plainly

On risk, without softening it

Synthetic indices are volatile by construction. Leveraged products can lose you more than you put in. Most people who trade these markets lose money.

Nothing on KonfluenX is investment advice, no signal is a recommendation, and past performance — including the win rates we work hard to keep honest — tells you about the past and nothing else.

Please read the Risk Disclosure and the Signal Disclaimer before you trade. Only ever risk money you can afford to lose.

Principles

Four rules we build against

Every design decision on this platform traces back to one of these.

Your money stays yours

No wallet, no balance, no custody. Deriv is the ledger and always will be.

The API decides, not the mockup

If Deriv cannot execute it, we do not offer it as a trade. No exceptions for a nicer screen.

Records are evidence-based

Signals lock on publish. Outcomes come from contracts and price feeds, never from claims.

The market is the meeting point

Trading, signals, learning and conversation all attach to a market — never floating free.
