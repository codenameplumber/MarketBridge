My Deriv balance looks wrong or out of date
The balance shown in KonfluenX is a cached read of your Deriv account with the time it was fetched underneath it. If it looks stale, pull to refresh on the dashboard, or open Settings → Connected Deriv Account and reconnect. Deriv is always the authority — if the two disagree, Deriv is right.
A signal I followed shows a different result to my trade
A signal's result is resolved from the contract or the price feed, not from your individual entry. If you entered at a different moment, took a different stake or used a different duration, your outcome will differ. Open the contract in Trade History to see exactly what your position did.
Why can't I trade a signal I am looking at?
It is an analysis signal. It carries entry, stop loss and target levels that do not map to a Deriv contract we can execute from here, so it shows View Market instead of a trade button. That is deliberate — we would rather tell you than give you a button that fails.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account Settings → Delete account, or email support and we will do it. Deleting removes your profile, posts and comments. Signals you published stay in aggregate form, without your identity attached, so that the win rates other people relied on when following you do not silently change. Deleting on KonfluenX does not close your Deriv account — do that with Deriv directly.
