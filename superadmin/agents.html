Deriv payment agents, and which of them your users see
Only ticked agents are shown to users. This list comes from Deriv’s payment agent directory — KonfluenX does not vet, endorse or transact with any of them, and money never moves through KonfluenX. Ticking an agent only decides whether their WhatsApp appears on the deposit screen.
Rates shown are indicative figures the agent last advertised. Users always confirm the live rate with the agent directly over WhatsApp before sending anything.
