Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026 · Version 1.0

What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. We have tried to write this in plain language rather than legal padding.

1. The short version

  • We collect what we need to run an account, a trading interface and a community — not more.
  • We never see or store your Deriv password. Connection uses Deriv's OAuth; we hold an encrypted access token you can revoke at any time.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Ever.
  • Your trading activity is not shown to other users unless you choose to publish it.
  • You can export or delete your data from Settings, or by asking us.

The rest of this page is the detail behind those five points.

2. What we collect

You give us

  • Account details — username, email, password (hashed, never stored in readable form), display name, and optionally an avatar, bio, country and strategy tags.
  • Content you publish — signals, analysis, posts, comments, reactions, charts and images you upload.
  • Support correspondence — messages you send us and our replies.
  • Preferences — theme, notification settings, watchlist, course progress.

Deriv gives us, with your authorisation

  • Your Deriv account identifiers, account type, currency and landing company.
  • Your account balance, read on demand and cached with a timestamp.
  • Contracts you open through KonfluenX, and their outcomes.
  • Not your Deriv password, your identity documents, your payment methods, or activity you carry out away from KonfluenX.

Collected automatically

  • Technical data — IP address, browser and device type, operating system, and the pages you visit on the platform.
  • Security data — login times, and the IP and user agent recorded against consent and disclaimer acknowledgements.
  • Local storage — theme choice, dismissed prompts and draft text stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your account — authentication, profile, settings, support.
  • To execute the trades you instruct — passing orders to Deriv and recording the result against your account.
  • To operate the community — showing your published content, follows, feeds and notifications.
  • To calculate performance honestly — resolving signal outcomes and computing win rates and rankings.
  • To keep the platform safe — detecting manipulation, coordinated accounts, spam and abuse; handling reports.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations — including retaining consent records and audit logs.
  • To improve the product — understanding which features are used, in aggregate.

Where the law requires a lawful basis, ours is: performance of our contract with you (running your account and executing your instructions), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, product improvement), legal obligation (record keeping), and consent (marketing email and push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time).

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

We share only what is necessary, with:

  • Deriv — the instructions required to authenticate you and to execute the trades you place.
  • Kaastro — only if you choose that deposit or withdrawal route, and only after the notice telling you that you are leaving KonfluenX. Kaastro then handles your data under their own policy.
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting, database, object storage, email delivery and error monitoring, all under contract and bound to process data only on our instruction.
  • Other users — anything you publish. Your username, avatar, bio, published signals, posts, comments, follower counts and performance statistics are public on the platform.
  • Authorities — where we are legally required to, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

What is never public: your email address, your Deriv account identifiers, your balance, your individual trades (unless you publish them), your reports of other users, and your support correspondence.

5. How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed with a modern, salted algorithm. We cannot read them.
  • Deriv access tokens are encrypted at rest, never written to logs, never returned by any API, and never rendered in a page.
  • Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS.
  • Access to production data is restricted to staff who need it, and administrative actions are logged in an append-only audit trail.
  • The browser's direct connection to Deriv for price data uses a read-scoped token. Trading permission stays server-side.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law, without undue delay.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data — while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of closure.
  • Published signals — retained in anonymised, aggregate form after account closure, so that the performance statistics other users relied on remain accurate.
  • Trading records — retained as long as required for dispute resolution and legal obligations, typically seven years.
  • Consent and disclaimer acknowledgements — retained as a legal record, with their version.
  • Audit logs — retained for security and compliance.
  • Support correspondence — two years.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is wrong — most of it you can edit yourself in Settings.
  • Delete your account and personal data, subject to the retention exceptions above.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing and notifications at any time.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

Exercise any of these from Settings, or write to privacy@vybe.app. We respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and local storage

We use the minimum necessary:

  • Essential cookies — session and security. These cannot be turned off without breaking login.
  • Local storage — your theme choice, dismissed prompts, unsent drafts and the offline cache. This never leaves your device.
  • Analytics — aggregate, privacy-respecting usage measurement. No cross-site tracking, no advertising pixels, no data broker relationships.

9. Children

KonfluenX is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us at privacy@vybe.app and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will notify you in the app and by email before material changes take effect. Every version is dated and versioned. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.

Questions: privacy@vybe.app.
