1. The general risk

Trading financial products carries a high level of risk. The value of an investment can fall as well as rise. You may not get back the amount you invested, and you may lose it all.

Most retail traders lose money trading leveraged products. You should trade only with capital you can afford to lose entirely without affecting your standard of living or your obligations.

If you do not fully understand a product, do not trade it. Ask, read, or practise on a demo account first.

2. Risks specific to synthetic indices

Synthetic indices — Volatility, Boom, Crash, Step, Jump — are simulated markets generated by an algorithm. They carry particular characteristics you need to understand:

  • They run continuously. 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. A position left open is exposed the whole time, and there is no closing bell to give you a natural stop.
  • Volatility is by design, not by accident. A Volatility 75 index is engineered to move sharply. Movements that would be extraordinary in a real market are routine here.
  • Boom and Crash indices spike. Boom indices produce frequent small downward ticks with occasional violent upward spikes; Crash indices do the reverse. A strategy that ignores the spike direction will be repeatedly stopped out.
  • No fundamentals exist. There is no news, no earnings, no macroeconomic reasoning. Analysis is purely technical and statistical.
  • Past patterns do not constrain future ones. The generator has no memory of what you observed yesterday.

3. Leverage and multipliers

Leveraged products, including Multipliers, magnify both gains and losses. A small adverse move in the underlying can produce a total loss of the amount you committed.

  • A multiplier of x100 means a 1% adverse move can wipe out your entire stake.
  • Stop losses and take profits reduce but do not eliminate risk — extreme moves can gap through a level.
  • Deal cancellation, where offered, is a paid feature with a time limit, not blanket protection.

4. Fixed-payout contract risks

Contracts such as Rise/Fall and Digits have a binary or near-binary outcome. If the condition is not met at expiry, you lose the entire stake — there is no partial recovery and no "close for a small loss".

Accumulators grow your stake with each tick that stays within a range, but a single tick outside that range ends the contract. The longer a position runs profitably, the more there is to lose on the tick that breaks it.

Payout percentages reflect probability. A high advertised payout signals a low-probability outcome, not a good opportunity.

5. Technology and execution risk

  • Connectivity. Internet, device or platform failure may prevent you from opening, monitoring or closing a position at the moment you want to.
  • Price movement between quote and execution. The price you see may not be the price you get.
  • Third-party dependency. KonfluenX relies on Deriv's API. Outages or changes there directly affect what you can do here.
  • Always have a fallback. You can reach your account and positions directly through Deriv at any time. Do not treat KonfluenX as your only route.

7. The risks that are not about markets

The most common cause of large trading losses is not analysis. It is behaviour.

  • Chasing losses. Increasing size after a loss to "win it back" is how an account ends.
  • Overtrading. 24/7 markets mean there is always another trade available. That is a trap, not an opportunity.
  • Overconfidence after a streak. A run of wins on a synthetic index is often variance, not skill.
  • Trading borrowed money. Never trade with money you need, owe, or borrowed.
  • Trading emotionally. If you are angry, desperate or trying to prove something, stop.

9. If trading stops being a choice

Trading can become compulsive. If you recognise any of the following, please stop and seek support:

  • Trading more than you planned, or more often than you intended.
  • Hiding your trading, or lying about losses.
  • Borrowing money to trade or to recover losses.
  • Trading affecting your sleep, work, mood or relationships.
  • Feeling unable to stop.

Support is available from organisations such as GamCare, Gamblers Anonymous and BeGambleAware, and from your local health service. You can also close your KonfluenX account at any time from Settings.
