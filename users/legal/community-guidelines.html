Community Guidelines

What we expect from every member, in every room.

Respect

No harassment, hate speech, threats or personal attacks. Disagree with the trade, not the trader.

No impersonation of other traders, firms, KonfluenX staff or Deriv.

Never ask anyone for a password, a seed phrase or money. KonfluenX connects to Deriv only through Deriv's own authorisation, and holds no trading funds.

Signals and analysis

Post signals only for the pair a market room is for. A V75 room takes V75 signals.

Entry, stop loss and targets lock when a signal is published. Results are worked out automatically from market data, not by votes.

If you think a result is wrong, use Dispute with your reason. A moderator reviews the price history and the calculation.

No promises of guaranteed profit, "risk-free" trades or pressure to copy a trade.

Fair participation

No spam, repeated messages, link farming or self-promotion outside your own room.

KX Points reward genuine help. Points earned through spam, self-engagement or fake activity are reversed.

Content from Pro rooms is for subscribers. Do not copy it out of the room.

Moderation

Moderators can flag messages and rooms. Admins review what is flagged and may remove content, mute rooms or suspend accounts.

Every moderation decision is recorded. If you think a decision was wrong, contact support.
